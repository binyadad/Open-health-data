# Health and Life Science Datasets

A free list of large data sources for health and life science research around the world, compiled for students, researchers and research groups. It covers population biobanks, genomic archives, health records, national registries, clinical trial data, imaging, global health surveys and the infrastructure that helps you find and access them. Some sources are fully open. Many individual-level datasets require registration, an application, training plus a data use agreement, or analysis inside a secure research environment. Suggestions and corrections are welcome: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

159 sources across 16 categories. Last updated: November 2026.

**Access terms used below:** Open (download freely); Open (registration) (free account needed); Controlled (application to a data access committee); Credentialed (training and a data use agreement); Secure enclave / TRE (analysis only inside a trusted research environment); Federated (your code runs where the data sit); Licensed / fee.

### Biobanks and cohorts

- [UK Biobank](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk) - WGS, genotypes, proteomics, imaging, linked hospital/death/cancer records, lifestyle

- [Our Future Health](https://ourfuturehealth.org.uk) - Genotypes, genetic ancestry, questionnaires, linked NHS data

### Genomics and omics

- [dbGaP](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gap/) - Genotype-phenotype studies (individual level)

- [European Genome-phenome Archive (EGA)](https://ega-archive.org) - Individual-level genomic and phenotype data
  * Coverage: Global (mainly Europe/Africa); Scale: ~thousands of studies; Access: Controlled (per-study DAC); Cost: Free
    + Federated EGA nodes in several countries incl. Norway
- [gnomAD](https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org) - Aggregated variant frequencies (exomes, genomes)
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~807,000 individuals (v4); Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [1000 Genomes / IGSR](https://www.internationalgenome.org) - Whole genomes, reference panels
  * Coverage: Global (26 populations); Scale: ~3,200 high-coverage genomes; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [TOPMed](https://topmed.nhlbi.nih.gov) - Deep WGS, omics, cohort phenotypes
  * Coverage: USA (multi-ancestry); Scale: ~200,000+ genomes; Access: Controlled (dbGaP/BioData Catalyst); open imputation server; Cost: Free
- [GWAS Catalog](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/) - Published GWAS associations and summary statistics
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~thousands of studies; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [PGS Catalog](https://www.pgscatalog.org) - Polygenic scores and metadata
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~thousands of scores; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [ClinVar](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/) - Variant-disease interpretations
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~millions of submissions; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [INSDC (SRA / ENA / DDBJ)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena) - Raw sequence reads and assemblies
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: Petabase scale; Access: Open; Cost: Free
    + Mirrored across NCBI SRA, ENA and DDBJ
- [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/) - Gene expression and functional genomics
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~millions of samples; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [ArrayExpress / BioStudies](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/arrayexpress) - Functional genomics experiments
  * Coverage: Global; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [GTEx](https://gtexportal.org) - Tissue expression and eQTLs
  * Coverage: USA; Scale: ~950 donors, 54 tissues; Access: Open (summary); controlled (individual); Cost: Free
- [ENCODE](https://www.encodeproject.org) - Regulatory elements, epigenomics
  * Coverage: Human and mouse; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [Human Cell Atlas Data Portal](https://data.humancellatlas.org) - Single-cell and spatial omics
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~tens of millions of cells; Access: Open (some managed access); Cost: Free
- [CZ CELLxGENE Discover](https://cellxgene.cziscience.com) - Curated single-cell datasets
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~tens of millions of cells; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [Open Targets Platform](https://platform.opentargets.org) - Integrated target-disease evidence
  * Coverage: Global; Access: Open; Cost: Free
- [Ensembl / UCSC Genome Browser](https://www.ensembl.org) - Reference genomes and annotations
  * Coverage: Global; Access: Open; Cost: Free
    + Also https://genome.ucsc.edu
- [International Mouse Phenotyping Consortium](https://www.mousephenotype.org) - Knockout mouse phenotypes
  * Coverage: Global; Scale: ~9,000+ genes; Access: Open; Cost: Free
